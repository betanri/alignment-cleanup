alignment-cleanup Pipeline

This repository documents a three-step cleanup workflow for shallow genomic alignments: custom trimming with locus dropping, TAPER masking, and a final custom flank masker aimed at terminal private mismatches / singleton edge junk.

Steps

  1. Occupancy Trim: occupancy-based edge trim, short-fragment masking, and keep/drop split.
  2. TAPER at cutoff 1.5 on the retained loci.
  3. Custom flank mask on the TAPER output.
